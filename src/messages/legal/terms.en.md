## Who can use the app

You need to be at least 18 years old to create an account yourself. Riders can also be added by a caretaker or by their chapter.

## Your account

Keep your sign-in methods, such as your email, phone and passkeys, to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else has used your account. Keep your details up to date so your chapter can reach you. If you add someone else as a passenger, you confirm that they, or their legal representative, agree.

## How we treat each other

The app connects passengers, pilots, caretakers and chapters. Be kind, respectful and honest. These are not allowed:

- harassment, threats or hate
- sexual, violent or otherwise inappropriate content
- spam and advertising
- sharing other people's personal details without their permission

## Reporting and blocking

You can report messages, profiles and people, and block anyone you no longer want to hear from. Chapter admins review every report and can remove content or suspend accounts.

Chapters review reports as soon as they can, usually within a few days. If you disagree with a decision, write to the contact below and we will look at it again.

## Rides and safety

- Only pilots who have completed their chapter's training may pilot a trishaw.
- Follow your chapter's guidelines, traffic rules and the instructions for the trishaw.
- Passengers and pilots decide together whether a ride is safe. Cancel or stop a ride if the weather, the road or someone's health makes it unsafe.
- Report damage to a trishaw and any accident to your chapter right away.

The chapter organising a ride is responsible for it. The app helps you plan rides; it does not replace your own judgement on the road.
