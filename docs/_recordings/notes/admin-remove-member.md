# admin-remove-member

- Account: chapterAdmin (Anke Admin)
- Start URL: /admin/members
- Data: "Tobias Brandl" (tobias.brandl@example.org), invited beforehand as a pilot, is removed. ("Lena Hofmann" from admin-invite-member was removed in a first take.)

1. On "Members", scroll "Everyone in the chapter" and click the member's name ("Tobias Brandl").
2. The member's page opens: name, email, phone ("No phone number"), History ("You sent an invitation", "An email went out — Invitation"). Right column "Roles" with the chapter, role "Pilot" and the buttons "Make chapter admin" and "Remove from chapter" (red). There is no ⋯ menu — the actions sit right there.
3. Click "Remove from chapter".
4. Confirmation: "Remove Tobias Brandl from the chapter?" — "Access to the chapter goes. The rides already ridden stay on the record." Buttons "Cancel" and "Remove from chapter".
5. Click "Remove from chapter". Toast "Tobias Brandl has left the chapter."
6. Back on "Members", the person is gone from "Everyone in the chapter" (count drops, here "1–15 of 15").

Notes:
- "Make chapter admin" → "Make {name} a chapter admin?" — "They can approve pilots, book rides and see everyone's details."
- Rules: you can't change your own role ("You can't change your own role. Ask another admin."), and a chapter can't lose its last admin.
- Known glitch: after confirming, the member page itself turns into a "404 — Page not found" screen (the admin no longer has access to that person) instead of returning to Members; the clip skips that moment by going back to Members.
