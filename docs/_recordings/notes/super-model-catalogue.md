# super-model-catalogue

- Account: country (Clara Country (DE))
- Start URL: /admin/trishaws/types
- Duration: ~22 s

## Steps
1. Trishaws › **Models** tab. Intro: "The trishaws your chapters ride, described once and shared as widely as you like — a chapter's own, the whole country's, or everyone's." Table columns: Model, Catalogue, Seats, Wheelchair, Trishaws. There is also a **Show archived** switch.
2. Click **New model**. A drawer titled **New model** opens: "Describe it once; every trishaw of this kind points here."
3. **Catalogue**: pick "Augsburg – Haus am Park — this chapter only". The options are each chapter ("— this chapter only") and "Deutschland — every chapter in the country". Hint: "Who sees it. A chapter model can be promoted to its country later."
4. **Name**: "Haus am Park Rikscha".
5. **Passenger seats**: "2 seats" (1–6).
6. Switch **Wheelchair accessible** on ("A wheelchair can be rolled on board.").
7. (Optional: Description, Photo, Manual PDF.)
8. Click **Add model**. Toast: "Haus am Park Rikscha is in the catalogue." The model's detail page opens: header badge "Chapter · Augsburg – Haus am Park · No trishaws use it". Properties: Seats, Wheelchair accessible, Catalogue, an **Archive** button. Sections: About this model, Photos, Manual.
9. In the **Catalogue** panel, click **Promote to Deutschland** (hint "Makes it available to more chapters. It cannot be narrowed again.").
10. The confirmation "Promote to Deutschland" opens with **Cancel** and **Promote to Deutschland**. Click **Cancel** (not promoted in the recording).

## Rules / notes seen on screen
- Catalogue levels: chapter only → country → Global ("Global — every chapter everywhere", superadmins only).
- Promotion is one-way: "It cannot be narrowed again."
- Models from a wider catalogue are read-only for chapter admins: "This model belongs to the Global catalogue. Only its admins can change it."
- A model that trishaws use can only be archived, not deleted.
