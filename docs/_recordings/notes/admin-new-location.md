# admin-new-location

- Account: chapterAdmin (Anke Admin)
- Start URL: /admin/locations
- Data created: location "Garage Lindwurmstraße" (Lindwurmstraße 88, 80337 München)

1. On "Locations" (sections "Your locations" — "Where your chapter's trishaws sleep." and "Pools you use"), click "New location" (top right; next to it "Join a pool").
2. Drawer "New location" — "A place where your chapter keeps trishaws."
3. "Name": "Garage Lindwurmstraße" (placeholder "Garage behind the care home"). The name is required ("Give the location a name.").
4. "Address": type "Lindwurmstraße 88, 80337 München" and pick the suggestion "Lindwurmstraße 88 80337 Munich, Germany". A map with a pin appears. Hint under the field: "Pick a result to place the pin; drag the pin to fine-tune."
5. "How to find the entrance": "Blue gate in the courtyard, second door on the right". (Optional "Photo of the entrance": "Upload photos", JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each, drag and drop works.)
6. "Access code": "4711" — hint "Door or key-safe code. Only admins and pilots see it."
7. ("Access notes" left empty — a small rich-text field with Bold/Italic/Strikethrough/Code/Link/List/Quote.)
8. "When bringing it back": "Plug in the charger, lock the back wheel and close the gate." (rich text too)
9. Click "Add location".
10. Toast "Garage Lindwurmstraße is ready." and the new location's detail page opens: header with "Chapter location" badge, sections "Access notes", "When bringing it back", "Entrance", "Place" (address + map), and a "Properties" panel (Kind, Belongs to, Access code 4711).

Notes:
- Everything on the detail page is editable in place (pencil icons).
