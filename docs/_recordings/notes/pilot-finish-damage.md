- Account: pilot@cwa.local
- Start URL: /pilot
- Ride: a pleasure ride Wed 14/10 14:00–15:30 with trishaw Isarwind (prepared via SQL, id docs-finish-ride-1)

1. On Home, tap the card **Finish your last ride** ("11h ago · Bring the trishaw back and tell us if anything broke.").
2. Page "Finish your ride": "Thanks for riding! Here is how to bring everything back." The "Your ride" box shows "Wed 14/10 · 14:00 – 15:30, München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof · Seniorenheim Sonnenhof".
3. Trishaw card **Isarwind** ("VeloPlus · 1 seat · Wheelchair"):
   - **Where it goes back**: "Depot Sonnenhof · Pool", "Sonnenstraße 12, 80331 München", **Open in Maps**
   - **Access code** 4711
   - **Entrance**: "Gate B on the courtyard side, next to the bike racks."
   - **Parking it**: "Plug the battery into the charger by the door and lock the gate."
   - Button **Report damage**
4. Scroll down and tap **Report damage**. A bottom sheet "Report damage on Isarwind" opens: "The chapter admins get your report straight away."
5. In **What is wrong?** (placeholder "Flat rear tyre, the left brake squeaks …") type "Flat rear tyre, the left brake squeaks."
   - Also in the sheet: **Photo** ("Upload photos", "JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each. Drop files here.") and the switch **Ground this trishaw** ("It can't be booked until an admin clears the damage. Use this when riding it would be unsafe."). The switch was left **off** in this clip.
6. Tap **Send report** (it reads "Sending…" for a moment). Toast: "Thanks — the chapter admins have been told." The sheet closes and the card now shows **You reported** with the report text and the time.
7. Scroll down and tap **Done**. Toast: "Thanks for riding!". The app returns to Home.

Notes:
- "Done" only closes the page. The "Finish your last ride" card stays on Home until 24 h after the ride ends.
- If no trishaw was allocated: "No trishaw was allocated to this ride, so there is nothing to bring back." If no return location is set: "No return location is set for this trishaw. Ask your chapter where it goes."
- Side effect: this left one open damage report on Isarwind (München fleet).
