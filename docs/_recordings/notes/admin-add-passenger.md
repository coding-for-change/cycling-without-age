# admin-add-passenger

- Account: chapterAdmin (Anke Admin)
- Start URL: /admin/passengers

1. On "Passengers", click "Add a passenger" (red button, top right).
2. Drawer "Add a passenger": "For someone who signs up at the door rather than on a phone. They can take the account over later with the same email or number."
3. "First name": "Erna". "Last name": "Huber".
4. "Birthday": 12 March 1938 (a date field; the recording browser shows it as mm/dd/yyyy — the order follows the browser's language).
5. "Gender": open the dropdown and pick "Female" (options Female / Male / Other).
6. "Email or phone number": "erna.huber@example.org".
7. Tick "Someone helps this person". A grey box appears with "Their name" ("Thomas Huber"), "Relationship" ("Son") and "Their email or phone number" ("thomas.huber@example.org"). All three are required once the box is ticked.
8. Click "Add passenger" (⌘↵ also works).
9. Toast: "Erna Huber is on the list." The drawer closes and Erna appears in the table (columns Name, Born, Joined) with her email under the name.

Notes:
- Switch "Add another right after" — "Keeps the form open for the next resident." (form clears, focus back on First name).
- If the helper's contact is the same as the passenger's contact, a hint appears: "This account will belong to {helper}, who manages {passenger}."
- Errors: "That email or number already has an account." / "Check the details — something isn't quite right."
