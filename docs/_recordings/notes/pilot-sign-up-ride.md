# pilot-sign-up-ride

- Account: pilot@cwa.local (Piet Pilot, approved in München + Hamburg)
- Start URL: /pilot

1. On Home, tap the **My rides** tab in the bottom bar.
2. "My rides" opens with the section "Open rides in München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof": "These rides still need a pilot. Take the ones that suit you." Each card shows the date and time, a "Needs 1 more pilot" badge, the ride type ("Functional ride"), the place, and the rider count ("1 rider"). Below that are "Coming up" (your own rides) and "Past rides".
3. Tap the open ride **Fri 16/10 · 10:00 – 11:00 · Functional ride**.
4. The open-ride page shows the "Needs 1 more pilot" badge, "Functional ride", "Fri 16/10 · 10:00 – 11:00 · tomorrow", "Seniorenheim Sonnenhof to Hausarzt Dr. Weber", and "1 rider". Then:
   - "München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof is looking for a pilot. Once you sign up you'll see who you're riding with, how to reach them and a chat with everyone on the ride."
   - A notice because the ride is inside the staffing window: "The ride is less than 48 hours away, so once you sign up, stepping back is a phone call to München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof."
5. Tap **Sign up for this ride**.
6. A toast reads "You're on the ride. Thank you!" and the page switches to the assigned view: "Ride chat" (Everyone on this ride, and your chapter.), "Where" (Start / Destination), and "Riders" with the rider's name and a phone button (tap-to-call).

Rules shown:
- Before signing up, a pilot sees no names or phone numbers, only when, where, the ride type and the rider count.
- The staffing window is 48 h by default (chapter setting). Inside it, a pilot can still sign up but can only step back by phoning the chapter.
- For a round trip: "Part of a round trip: you'll pilot the way back too, {time}, if it still needs a pilot."
