# pilot-chat

- Account: pilot@cwa.local
- Start URL: /pilot

1. On Home, tap the **Chat** tab in the bottom bar.
2. "Chat" lists the conversations. At the top: the "Unreads" switch, the **New** button, and the search field "Search names and groups". Every ride Piet pilots has a group chat named after the ride ("Sommerfest im Sonnenhof", "Ausfahrt in den Englischen Garten", "Seniorenheim Sonnenhof", "Rund um die Alster" …).
3. Tap **Ausfahrt in den Englischen Garten**.
4. The thread opens with the header "Ausfahrt in den Englischen Garten · 2 in this group" and a back arrow ("All chats").
5. Tap **Write a message** and type "Hi everyone! I'll be at the Chinese Tower at 14:45 with Isarwind."
6. Tap the red **Send** button. The message appears under a date separator, with the time and a delivered tick.

Notes:
- A ride chat includes everyone on the ride (pilots, riders or the person who books for them) and the chapter.
