# admin-damage

- Account: chapterAdmin (Anke Admin)
- Start URL: /admin/trishaws
- Data: trishaw "Föhn" (ends back at status Ready, no open damage; history keeps the report).

1. On "Trishaws", click "Föhn". Its detail page opens; the "Damage" box on the right says "No open damage. Ready to ride as far as anyone knows."
2. Click "Report damage". Drawer "Report damage" — "Tell the other admins what is wrong with Föhn."
3. "What is wrong?": "Front brake cable frayed, brakes feel soft." (optional "Photo": "Upload photos").
4. Switch on "Ground this trishaw" — "It can't be booked until an admin clears the damage. Use this when riding it would be unsafe." The send button turns red.
5. Click "Send report".
6. Toast "Thanks — the chapter admins have been told." A red "Grounded" badge appears under the name, Status switches to "In the workshop" automatically, the Damage box lists the report ("Grounded · date"), the calendar row shows "In for service", and History gets "You changed the status from Ready to In the workshop" and "You reported damage" (badges "Open", "Grounded", button "Clear").
7. In History, click "Clear" on the report. Dialog "Clear this damage" shows the report text.
8. "What happened?": "New brake cable fitted at the bike shop." (placeholder "Fixed, new tyre fitted · Unjustified"). Click "Clear damage" ("Cancel" backs out).
9. Toast "Damage cleared." The Grounded badge and the open report are gone ("No open damage…"), but Status is still "In the workshop" — clearing does not change the status.
10. In "About" → "Status", choose "Ready" (options: Ready, In the workshop, Retired). The pill turns green "Ready"; it saves immediately.

Notes:
- Without "Ground this trishaw" the report is informational: the trishaw stays bookable and the list shows "Damage reported".
