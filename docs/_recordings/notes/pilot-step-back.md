# pilot-step-back

- Account: pilot@cwa.local
- Start URL: /pilot/rides

Part 1: outside the staffing window
1. On "My rides", tap **Sun 18/10 · 16:00 – 18:00 · Pleasure ride · Seniorenheim Sonnenhof** (in 3 days).
2. The ride page shows "Scheduled", "Pleasure ride", "Sun 18/10 · 16:00 – 18:00 · in 3d", Ride chat, Where, Riders ("Nobody is booked yet."), Trishaws (Isarwind), and Riding with you ("Just you on this one.").
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Step back from this ride**.
4. A confirmation dialog opens: "Step back from this ride?", "Your chapter will be told and can look for someone else." with the buttons **Step back** and **Keep my seat**. Tap **Step back**.
5. Toast: "You've stepped back. Your chapter knows." The page turns into the open-ride view ("Needs 1 more pilot", "Sign up for this ride"), so the ride is open for other pilots again.

Part 2: inside the staffing window
6. Back on "My rides", tap **Thu 15/10 · 15:00 – 17:00 · Englischer Garten** (in 13 h).
7. Scroll to the bottom. Instead of a step-back button there is the notice: "With less than 48 hours to go, stepping back is a phone call: please ring München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof."

Rules shown:
- The staffing window is 48 h before the start (chapter setting `staffingCheckLeadHours`, default 48).
- For a round trip, the dialog reads: "You'll step back from both legs of the round trip, as far as there is still time. Your chapter will be told and can look for someone else."
