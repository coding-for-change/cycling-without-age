# admin-approve-pilot

- Account: chapterAdmin (Anke Admin)
- Start URL: /admin/rides

1. Click "Members" in the sidebar.
2. The section "Waiting for approval" (badge "1 waiting") explains: "Everyone here asked to pilot for your chapter. Read them, decide, and they hear from us the same minute." The row shows "Pernille Pending", pilot.pending@cwa.local, asked date, and the buttons "Approve" and "Turn down".
3. Click "Approve" in Pernille's row. A drawer opens: title "Welcome Pernille Pending aboard?", text "They get an email with the three steps to their first ride."
4. In "A note for Pernille Pending (optional)" type: "Welcome! Training ride is Saturday at 10:00 at the Sonnenhof garage." (max 500 characters; placeholder "A training date, a phone number, a friendly word.")
5. Click "Approve" (bottom of the drawer; ⌘↵ also submits).
6. Toast: "Pernille Pending is aboard." The "Waiting for approval" section disappears (it only shows while someone is waiting) and Pernille is now in "Everyone in the chapter" as a Pilot.

Notes:
- "Turn down" opens the same drawer titled "Turn {name} down?" — "They get an email, and can ask another chapter instead." Toast: "{name} has been told."
