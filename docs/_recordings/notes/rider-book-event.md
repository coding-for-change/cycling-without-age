- Account: caretaker@cwa.local (Carla Caretaker, books for Inge Caretaker and Walter Caretaker)
- Start URL: /passenger
- Event: "Ausfahrt in den Englischen Garten", Thu 15/10 15:00–17:00, 8 seats, "Riders can book" switched on

1. Home ("Hi Carla") shows **Upcoming events** with the card "Thu 15/10 · 15:00 – 17:00 · Ausfahrt in den Englischen Garten · Englischer Garten · 8 seats left". Tap the card.
2. The event page shows the badge "Event", the title, "Thu 15/10 · 15:00 – 17:00", the chapter, the place "Englischer Garten", and "8 seats left".
3. Section **Book a seat**: "Pick who you'd like to bring along. The chapter takes care of the rest." There is one checkbox per rider the account books for. Tick **Inge Caretaker** and **Walter Caretaker**.
4. The button changes from "Pick who's coming" (disabled) to **Book 2 seats**. Tap it.
5. Toast: "2 seats booked. We look forward to riding with you!" The page becomes the booked ride view: "Who's riding" (Inge Caretaker, Walter Caretaker), "Your pilot" (Piet Pilot), "Your chapter", and "Questions about this ride? … is happy to help."

Rules shown or known:
- Only events the chapter marked "Riders can book" appear, and only while there are seats left.
- Too many riders picked: "Only # seats left, pick up to #". Closed event: "This event isn't taking bookings anymore."
- An account that books for only itself sees just its own name.
