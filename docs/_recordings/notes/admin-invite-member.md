# admin-invite-member

- Account: chapterAdmin (Anke Admin)
- Start URL: /admin/members

1. On "Members", click "Invite someone" (top right).
2. Drawer "Invite someone" opens: "They get an email with a link to join München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof."
3. "Their name": type "Lena Hofmann" (placeholder "Anna Schmidt").
4. "Email address": type "lena.hofmann@example.org". An avatar preview appears next to the fields as soon as the email is typed.
5. "Role" offers two checkboxes: "Chapter admin" — "Runs the chapter, approves new pilots and invites people." and "Pilot" — "Does the pedalling and takes passengers out for a ride." "Pilot" is ticked by default; both can be ticked; at least one is required (the send button is disabled otherwise).
6. Click "Send the invitation" (⌘↵ also works). "Cancel" closes without sending.
7. Toast: "Invitation sent to Lena Hofmann." Lena appears straight away in "Everyone in the chapter" with role "Pilot".

Notes:
- Error texts: "Check the name and the email address." / "That didn't work. Try again."
