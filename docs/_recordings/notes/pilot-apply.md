# pilot-apply

- Account: new account new.pilot.docs@cwa.local (becomes "Paula Pedal")
- Start URL: /sign-in (location permission granted, device placed in central München)

1. Screen "What's your email or phone number?". Type `new.pilot.docs@cwa.local` and tap **Continue**.
2. Screen "Enter your code". Type the 6-digit code from the email. It submits by itself.
3. Screen "How would you like to take part?" with two cards: "I want to be a Passenger" (You sit at the front and enjoy the wind in your hair.) and "I want to be a Pilot" (You do the pedalling and take passengers out for a ride.). Tap **I want to be a Pilot**.
4. Screen "Where would you like to ride?" with the subtitle "Chapters closest to you.". There is a map of chapters, a search field "Chapter or city", and a list with distances ("München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof · 770 m away", "Augsburg – Haus am Park · 54 km away" …). The hint "Select a chapter to continue." sits under the list.
5. Tap **München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof**. It gets a check mark and the map zooms to it.
6. Tap **Request to join**. While it saves, the button reads "Just a moment…".
7. Screen "Two quick agreements". Tick both:
   - "I agree to receive email and push notifications for important messages."
   - "I agree to the storage and processing of my data. See the Imprint and the Data processing agreement."
   - Until both are ticked: "Tick all of them to continue."
8. Tap **Continue**.
9. Screen "A little about you": "Your chapter needs this to plan a ride. Nothing else, for now." Fill in **First name** `Paula`, **Last name** `Pedal`, **Birthday** (date picker, 10 May 1975) and **Gender** (Female / Male / Other → Female). Tap **Continue**. The button stays disabled until every field is filled.
10. Screen "Create your passkey": "Your device remembers you — no password to keep." It has the field "Name this passkey (optional)" and the buttons "Create passkey" and "Not now". Tap **Not now**.
11. Screen "How you become a pilot" with three steps:
    1. "Watch the training videos — about 20 minutes, right in this app."
    2. "Join a practical workshop with one of the captains."
    3. "Grab your first ride — a captain rides along the first time."
    Tap **Next**.
12. Home: "Hi Paula". A pending tracker card reads "Your request is with München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof", "Asked on <date>", with the steps Applied → 2 Under review → 3 Welcome, "Being read by" (the admin, here "Anke"), and "Chapters usually answer within a few days. Nothing for you to do." Below it: "Next ride" (empty) and "Training while you wait".

Rules shown:
- Up to a limited number of chapters can be requested at once (error copy: "You can request up to {count} chapters at once.").
- A passkey can be added later under Account.

Notes for the writer:
- The headless browser displays the birthday as mm/dd/yyyy (05/10/1975). On a real phone the date picker follows the device's format.
- This clip runs about 54 s because it covers every onboarding screen. Cut or speed it up if you need it shorter.
