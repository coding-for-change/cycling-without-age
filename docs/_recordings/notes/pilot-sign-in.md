# pilot-sign-in

- Account: pilot@cwa.local (Piet Pilot), signed out at start
- Start URL: /welcome
- Viewport: iPhone 14 (mobile)

1. The welcome screen shows the carousel ("Everyone has the right to wind in their hair.", 4 steps), the language pill "EN", the heading "Come along for the ride.", and the buttons "Sign in" and "Explore without signing in".
2. Tap **Sign in**.
3. Screen "What's your email or phone number?". Type `pilot@cwa.local` into the field (placeholder "you@example.com"). The other options are "Use a passkey" and "Continue with Google".
4. Tap **Continue**.
5. Screen "Enter your code": "We emailed a 6-digit code to pilot@cwa.local." Type the 6-digit code from the email into the boxes. It submits by itself once the sixth digit is in. No button press is needed.
   - Below: "You can ask for a new code in a moment." (it becomes "Send a new code") and "Use a different email or number".
6. After a short blank page load, the pilot lands on Home: "Hi Piet", "Ready when you are.", the "Find open rides" button, the "Finish your last ride" card, and "Coming up".

Rules shown or known:
- Sign-in codes are limited to 3 sends per minute. A fourth shows "Too many attempts. Try again in a minute."
- A wrong code shows "That code isn't right. Check it and try again."
