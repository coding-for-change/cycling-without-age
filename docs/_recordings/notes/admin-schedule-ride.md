- Account: chapterAdmin (Anke Admin)
- Start URL: /admin/rides (week calendar)
- Data created: Pleasure ride Sat 17/10/2026 10:00–11:00 from "München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof", trishaw Zugspitze, passenger Erna Huber, pilot Pernille Pending (an earlier take left an identical ride at 14:30 the same day).

1. On "Rides" (toggle "Calendar" / "List", week arrows, "Today", "Week of Mon 12/10"), click "Schedule a ride" (red, top right; also in the sidebar and in ⌘K).
2. The drawer "New ride" opens (breadcrumb "München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof › New ride"). Three ride models at the top: "Event ride", "Pleasure ride", "Functional ride". Click "Pleasure ride" — "A ride for the joy of it — wind in the hair, back where it began."
3. Click the time row (shows date, start–end, "Europe/Berlin · 1 hr"). It expands into three buttons: date, start, end.
4. Date: click it and pick Saturday 17 October in the month calendar.
5. Start: click it and type "10:00", Enter (a 15-minute list is also offered). The end moves along automatically (one hour later, 11:00).
6. "Where it starts": click and pick "München – Seniorenheim Sonnenhof — Chapter location". A map with the pick-up pin appears.
7. "Trishaws": click "Add trishaws" ("A pleasure ride uses one trishaw."; counter 0 / 1) and pick "Zugspitze" (list shows model, seats, location; pool trishaws carry a "Pool" badge, damaged ones "Damage reported").
8. "Passengers": click "Add passengers" (counter 0 / 2 — seats of the chosen trishaw), type "Erna" in "Search by name", tick "Erna Huber", Esc.
9. "Pilots": click "Add pilots" (0 / 1), type "Pernille", tick "Pernille Pending", Esc.
10. (Optional "Note for pilots" — not used here.) The footer reads "1 trishaw selected" (without one: "No trishaw selected — the ride goes out without one.").
11. Press ⌘↵ (the button "Schedule ride ⌘↵").
12. Toast "Ride scheduled." and the ride's detail page opens ("Pleasure ride · Sat 17/10", badge "Scheduled", Riders, Note for pilots, History: "You scheduled the ride", "You booked Erna Huber", "You put Pernille Pending on as pilot"; Properties, Where, Trishaws on the right).
13. Click "All rides" — the week calendar shows the new ride on Sat 17/10 at 10:00 ("Zugspitze · 1 rider").

Notes:
- Event ride adds Event title, Description, Pilots needed (+/−), Capacity, "Riders can book". Functional ride is a trip to a destination (and back).
