Every ride has its own chat, and you can write to anyone who shares a chapter with you.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="mx-auto w-full max-w-xs rounded-3xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/pilot-chat.mp4" />

## Write in a ride chat

1. Tap **Chat** in the bottom bar.
2. Tap the ride's chat. It carries the ride's name.
3. Tap **Write a message**, type, and tap **Send**.

You can also open it from the ride page with **Ride chat**.

## Start a new chat

1. In **Chat**, tap **New**.
2. Pick **Person** or **Group** and search by name, email or phone number.
3. Tap **Start chat**, or **Create group** for a group.

<Note>
You can only start a chat with people who share a chapter with you.
</Note>

## Good to know

- A ride chat opens once a pilot is on the ride. It holds the pilots, the riders' accounts and your chapter's admins.
- On an event, the ride chat is for pilots and admins only.
- A message can be up to 4000 characters.
