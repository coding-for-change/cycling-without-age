You ask a chapter to join, and one of its admins answers. Anyone with an account can ask.

## Steps

1. [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in) with your email or phone number.
2. Tap **I want to be a Pilot**.
3. Pick the chapters you'd like to ride with. The closest come first, or search by **Chapter or city**.
4. Tap **Request to join**.
5. Read the short note on privacy and tap **Continue**.
6. Fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Birthday** and **Gender**, then tap **Continue**. Your chapter needs these to plan a ride.
7. Tap **Create passkey**, or **Not now** to do it later.
8. Read **How you become a pilot** and tap **Next**.

Home now shows **Your request is with** your chapter, with the steps **Applied**, **Under review** and **Welcome**. Chapters usually answer within a few days. There is nothing for you to do meanwhile.

<Note>
You can ask up to 5 chapters at once.
</Note>

## When the chapter answers

- **Yes**: Home shows **You're in.** and you can [sign up for rides](/en/pilots/sign-up-for-a-ride).
- **No**: Home shows that the chapter said no this time, with the admin's note under **Their note**. Tap **Find another chapter** to ask somewhere else.

Either way, you also get an email.

## Good to know

- Skipped the passkey? CWA GO asks again after 30 days. You can add one any time under [Your account](/en/getting-started/your-account).
- A chapter's poster or join link has an **Ask to pilot here** button that takes you straight to that chapter.
