Everything for the ride is on its page: where to go, the trishaw's access code, and your riders' phone numbers.

## Steps

1. On Home, under **Coming up**, tap your ride.
2. Under **Where**, tap **Open in Maps** to find the start.
3. Under **Picking up the trishaw**, find where it waits, the **Access code** and the **Entrance**.
4. Under **Riders**, tap the phone button to call a rider.
5. Tap **Ride chat** to write to everyone on the ride.

<Note>
The trishaw card with the access code shows up to 24 hours before the ride starts.
</Note>

## Good to know

- A rider booked by someone else shows "via" and that person's name. The phone button calls them.
- **Riding with you** lists the other pilots on the ride.
- If the ride changes, you get a notification. A cancellation always comes by email too, in case you are already on your way.
