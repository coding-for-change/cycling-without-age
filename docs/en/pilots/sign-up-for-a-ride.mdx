Open rides are rides your chapter still needs a pilot for. You can take any that suit you.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="mx-auto w-full max-w-xs rounded-3xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/pilot-sign-up-ride.mp4" />

## Steps

1. Tap **My rides** in the bottom bar.
2. Under **Open rides**, tap a ride. You see when and where it is, the ride type and how many riders it has.
3. Tap **Sign up for this ride**.

You see **You're on the ride. Thank you!** The page now shows the riders, how to reach them and the **Ride chat**. See [Ride day](/en/pilots/ride-day).

<Note>
Only chapters where **Pilots sign up themselves** is switched on show open rides. Otherwise your chapter puts you on rides itself.
</Note>

## Good to know

- Open rides cover the next 60 days.
- Rider names and phone numbers only show once you have signed up.
- A round trip is two rides. Signing up for one leg takes the other too, if it still needs a pilot.
- **Someone was quicker** means another pilot took the last place first.
- Inside the staffing window you can still sign up, but stepping back is then a phone call. See [Stepping back](/en/pilots/stepping-back).
