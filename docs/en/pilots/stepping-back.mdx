If you can't make a ride, you step back in the app. Your chapter is told and can look for someone else.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="mx-auto w-full max-w-xs rounded-3xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/pilot-step-back.mp4" />

## Steps

1. Tap **My rides** and open the ride.
2. Scroll down and tap **Step back from this ride**.
3. Tap **Step back** to confirm.

You see **You've stepped back. Your chapter knows.** The ride is open to other pilots again.

<Warning>
Close to the ride, stepping back is a phone call. This staffing window is set by your chapter: 12, 24, 48 or 72 hours, or 7 days before the start. The default is 48 hours. Inside it, the page tells you to ring your chapter.
</Warning>

## Good to know

- On a round trip you step back from both legs, as far as there is still time.
- You can still sign up for a ride inside the staffing window. The ride page warns you first.
