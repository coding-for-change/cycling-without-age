After the ride you bring the trishaw back and tell your chapter if anything broke.

## Bring the trishaw back

1. On Home, tap **Finish your last ride**.
2. Each trishaw card shows **Where it goes back**, the **Access code**, the **Entrance** and **Parking it**. Tap **Open in Maps** for directions.
3. Tap **Done** when you're finished.

## Report damage

1. On the trishaw's card, tap **Report damage**.
2. Under **What is wrong?**, describe the problem (3 to 2000 characters).
3. Add a **Photo** if it helps.
4. If riding it would be unsafe, switch on **Ground this trishaw**.
5. Tap **Send report**.

The chapter admins are told straight away. The card now shows **You reported**.

<Warning>
A grounded trishaw can't be booked until an admin clears the damage.
</Warning>

## Good to know

- **Finish your last ride** is there from the start of the ride until 24 hours after it ends.
- Report each trishaw on its own card.
- No return place set? Ask your chapter where the trishaw goes.
