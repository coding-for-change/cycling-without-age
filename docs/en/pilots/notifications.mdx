CWA GO tells you when something about your rides or chapter changes. You choose the extras.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="mx-auto w-full max-w-xs rounded-3xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/account-notifications.mp4" />

## Steps

1. Tap your avatar to open your account.
2. Scroll to **Notifications**.
3. Switch on or off what you want:
   - **Push**: a tap on your phone when something happens.
   - **Email**: the same news, in your inbox.
   - **Messages**: a buzz when someone writes, unless you have that chat open.
   - **Messages by email**: one mail with what you missed, only when your phone can't be reached.

<Note>
The essentials always reach you, such as a ride confirmed or a chapter answering. The switches are for the extras.
</Note>

## Good to know

- Push only works in the [app](/en/getting-started/install-the-app), not in the browser.
- If a push can't reach your phone, the same news comes by email about 2 minutes later.
- A cancelled ride always comes by email too.
- **Messages by email** waits until a chat goes quiet for about 5 minutes, and sends at most 15 minutes after the first unread message.
