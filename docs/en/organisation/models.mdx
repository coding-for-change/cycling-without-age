A model describes one kind of trishaw: seats, wheelchair access, photos and the manual. Every trishaw of that kind points to it, so you write it down once.

## Who sees a model

Each model lives in one catalogue:

- **this chapter only**: one chapter. Its chapter admins manage it.
- **every chapter in the country**: managed by the country admins.
- **Global — every chapter everywhere**: managed by superadmins.

Chapter admins can use a wider model for their trishaws, but can't change it.

## Add a model

1. Open **Trishaws** in the sidebar and switch to the **Models** tab.
2. Click **New model**.
3. Under **Catalogue**, pick who should see it.
4. Enter the **Name** and the number of **Passenger seats**.
5. Switch on **Wheelchair accessible** if a wheelchair can be rolled on board.
6. Add a **Description**, a **Photo** and the **Manual** as a PDF if you have them.
7. Click **Add model**.

## Share it more widely

1. Open the model.
2. In the **Catalogue** panel, click **Promote to** and the country, or **Promote to global**.
3. Confirm.

<Warning>
Promotion can't be undone. A model never goes back to a narrower catalogue.
</Warning>

## Good to know

- **Archive** hides a model from the list for new trishaws. Trishaws that use it keep it. Switch on **Show archived** to find it, then click **Restore**.
- You can only delete a model that no trishaw uses. Otherwise archive it.
- Only superadmins promote a model to global.
