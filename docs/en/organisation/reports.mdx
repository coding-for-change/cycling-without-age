Reports show what your chapters rode, for any timeframe and scope. Country admins see their countries, superadmins everything.

## Read the report

1. Open **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Pick a **Timeframe**: **7D**, **30D**, **90D**, **YTD**, **12M**, **All**, or **Custom** for your own dates.
3. Pick the **Scope** next to it: all chapters, one country or one chapter.
4. Read the key figures at the top: **Rides**, **Trips**, **Riders**, **Hours** and **Cancellations**. Each compares with the period before.
5. Click a figure to show it in **Activity over time**.
6. In the chart, click a bar to zoom in on that period.

## Compare and drill in

1. **Where the rides happened** shows each chapter as a bubble. The bigger the bubble, the more rides.
2. The **Leaderboard** ranks **Chapters**, and **Countries** when your scope spans several. Switch between **Rides**, **Hours** and **Per pilot**.
3. Click **See all** for the full table. You can sort it by any column.
4. Click a chapter on the map or in the leaderboard. The report switches to that chapter.

In a single chapter, the leaderboard ranks **Pilots** and **Riders** instead.

## Good to know

- **Cancellations** shows why trips were cancelled. **Ride models** shows how rides split across event, pleasure and functional rides.
- **People** counts active and inactive pilots and riders. Active means at least one ride in the last 12 months, whatever timeframe you picked.
- There is no export. Share the page link instead: it keeps the timeframe.
