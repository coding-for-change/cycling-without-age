A pool is a depot that several chapters of one country share. Country admins and superadmins start it, hand out its code and decide who gets in.

## Start a pool

1. Open **Locations** in the sidebar and switch to the **Pools** tab.
2. Click **New pool**.
3. Give it a **Name**.
4. In **Address**, type the street and pick the result. Drag the pin to fine-tune.
5. Add **How to find the entrance**, so pilots find the door on their first visit.
6. Fill in what helps: **Photo of the entrance**, **Access code**, **Access notes** and **When bringing it back**.
7. Click **Start pool**.

The pool's page opens. Its **Pool code** sits in the right column. Click **Copy** and send it to the chapters that should join.

## Let chapters in

A chapter admin joins under **Locations** with **Join a pool**: they enter the **Pool code** and click **Send request**.

1. Open the pool. Requests wait under **Waiting for you**.
2. Click **Approve**, or **Decline**.
3. Add a note if you like and confirm with **Approve**.

The chapter's admins now see the access notes and can book the pool's trishaws. Until you answer, they can't.

## Good to know

- **Members may manage trishaws** is off by default. Switch it on and admins of member chapters may add, edit and move the pool's trishaws.
- **New code** replaces the code. The old one stops working, chapters already in stay in.
- Only chapters in the pool's country can join.
- You can delete a pool only when no trishaws are left in it. Every member chapter loses access.
