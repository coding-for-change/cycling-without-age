A country groups its chapters and gets one or more country admins. Only superadmins can do this.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/super-new-country.mp4" />

## Add a country

1. Open **Countries** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New country**.
3. Start typing in **Country** and pick it from the list. The name and code fill in by themselves.
4. Click **Save country**.

## Give it a country admin

1. In the country's row, click **Add admin**.
2. Enter their **Name** and **Email address** and click **Add a country admin**.

They get an invitation by email and in their notifications, and their name shows with **invited** in the **Country admins** column. Once they accept it, the name stands there on its own. To withdraw an invitation or remove an admin, click the name and confirm.

<Note>
Nobody needs an account beforehand: CWA GO creates one for a new address. The role only becomes active once the person accepts the invitation, signed in with a passkey. The invitation runs out after 7 days.
</Note>

## Good to know

- **Admins** in the sidebar lists every superadmin and, per country, its country admins. Superadmins are invited and removed there; removing or inviting one asks for your passkey, and the last superadmin can't be removed.
- A country admin can open chapters, run trishaw pools and act as chapter admin for every chapter in the country.
- Deleting a country deletes every chapter in it, with all their rides and trishaws. See [Deleting things](/en/organisation/deleting).
