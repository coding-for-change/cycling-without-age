A country groups its chapters and gets one or more country admins. Only superadmins can do this.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/super-new-country.mp4" />

## Add a country

1. Open **Countries** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New country**.
3. Start typing in **Country** and pick it from the list. The name and code fill in by themselves.
4. Click **Save country**.

## Give it a country admin

1. In the country's row, click **Add admin**.
2. Enter their **Email address** and click **Add a country admin**.

Their name now shows in the **Country admins** column. To remove them, click their name and confirm.

<Note>
The person needs an account first. Ask them to sign in to CWA GO once, then add them.
</Note>

## Good to know

- A country admin can open chapters, run trishaw pools and act as chapter admin for every chapter in the country.
- Deleting a country deletes every chapter in it, with all their rides and trishaws. See [Deleting things](/en/organisation/deleting).
