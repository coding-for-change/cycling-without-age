The scope decides which chapters every admin page shows. Country admins and superadmins can switch it at any time.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/super-scope-switch.mp4" />

## What a scope is

- **All chapters**: every chapter you can reach. For a superadmin, that is every chapter everywhere.
- **All chapters in** a country: only that country's chapters.
- One chapter by name: only that chapter.

The breadcrumb at the top always shows the current scope, for example "All chapters in Deutschland › Members".

## Switch the scope

1. Click the button at the top of the sidebar. It shows your current scope and your role.
2. Under **Scope**, pick **All chapters**, a country or a chapter.

The page you are on stays open and shows only the chapters you picked. Members, Rides, Trishaws, Reports and every other admin page follow the same scope. CWA GO remembers it for your next visit.

You can also press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows) and type a chapter or country name. The **Scope** group holds the same entries as the sidebar.

<Tip>
A link with `?chapter=` and the chapter's web address at the end opens a page straight in that chapter, for example `/admin/rides?chapter=augsburg`. Handy to share with a colleague.
</Tip>

## Some buttons need one chapter

Anything that belongs to a single chapter only appears when one chapter is selected:

- **Invite someone** on Members
- **Add a passenger** on Passengers
- The chapter's settings on **Settings**

If you miss a button, pick the chapter in the sidebar first.

## Good to know

- A country admin sees only their own countries and those countries' chapters.
- Are you also a pilot or a rider? Then the same sidebar menu shows **Perspective**. Pick **Pilot** or **Passenger** to open the app as they see it, and **Admin** to come back. In ⌘K it's **View as pilot** and **View as passenger**.
