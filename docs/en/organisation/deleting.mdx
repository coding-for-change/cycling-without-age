Deleting is permanent. Country admins can delete chapters in their country. Only superadmins delete countries and people.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/super-delete-chapter.mp4" />

## Delete a chapter

1. Open **Chapters** and click the chapter.
2. Click the trash icon in the header (**Delete chapter**).
3. Read the red box. It lists what goes with the chapter, for example "3 trishaws" or "12 rides".
4. Type the word shown, for example `DELETE-AUGSBURG-HAUS-AM-PARK`. Upper or lower case doesn't matter.
5. Click **Delete chapter**.

A country works the same way: open **Countries** and click the trash icon in its row. Deleting a country deletes every chapter in it, and everything that belongs to them.

<Warning>
Nothing deleted can be brought back. Not the chapter, not its rides, not its trishaws.
</Warning>

## Delete a person

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/super-delete-user.mp4" />

Only superadmins can do this.

1. Open **Members** and search for the person in the **Person** column.
2. Click their name.
3. Click the trash icon in the header (**Delete user**).
4. Type the word shown, for example `DELETE-ANNA-AUGSBURG`.
5. Click **Delete user**.

Their account, roles, requests and history are gone. Rides already ridden stay on the record.

## Good to know

- You can't delete your own account here.
- To leave CWA GO yourself as an admin, hand over your admin role to someone else first. Then **Delete account** under **Sign out or delete** in your account works for you too.
- To take someone out of one chapter only, use **Remove from chapter** on their page instead. See [Offboarding](/en/chapter-admins/offboarding).
