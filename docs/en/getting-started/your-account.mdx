Your account holds your details and how CWA GO reaches you. Everyone has one.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="mx-auto w-full max-w-xs rounded-3xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/account-notifications.mp4" />

## Open your account

Tap your avatar at the top left. The **Account** sheet slides up. Changes save by themselves, and a toast offers **Undo**.

## What you find there

- **Profile**: your name, birthday and gender.
- **Language**: English, Dansk or Deutsch. Emails and notifications come in this language too.
- **Notifications**: four switches for push and email. See [Notifications](/en/pilots/notifications).
- **Calendar**: tap **Create my calendar link** to see your rides in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook. New rides turn up there, moved ones move, cancelled ones say so.
- **Passkeys**: tap **Add a passkey** so this device signs you in without a code.
- **Something not working?**: tap **Report a problem** and tell us what happened in a sentence or two.
- **Sign out or delete**: sign out of this device, or delete your account.

<Warning>
Your calendar link is private. Anyone who has it can see when and where your rides are. If it gets out, tap **New link**, or **Turn off** to stop it altogether.
</Warning>

## Delete your account

Tap **Delete account**, type **DELETE** and confirm. Your account, roles and history are removed. Rides you have already ridden stay on the record.

<Note>
While you still run a chapter or a country, you can't delete your account. Hand your admin role to someone else first.
</Note>
