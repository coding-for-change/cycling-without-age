You sign in without a password. CWA GO sends you a code, or your device signs you in with a passkey.

## Sign in with a code

1. Open CWA GO and tap **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email address or phone number and tap **Continue**.
3. Type the 6-digit code from the email or text message. It signs you in by itself once the last digit is in.

No code? Wait a moment and tap **Send a new code**. Typed the wrong address? Tap **Use a different email or number**.

## Sign in with a passkey

If you have added a passkey, tap **Use a passkey** on the first screen. Your phone or computer asks for your fingerprint, face or PIN. See [Your account](/en/getting-started/your-account) to add one.

<Note>
A code works for 10 minutes. You can ask for at most 3 codes a minute. After that you see **Too many attempts. Try again in a minute.**
</Note>

## Good to know

- You stay signed in on a device for 90 days after you last used CWA GO.
- You can also tap **Continue with Google**. In the iPhone app you can also tap **Continue with Apple**.
- When the terms of use change, you see **We've updated our terms of use** the next time you sign in. Tick **I accept the terms of use** and tap **Continue**.
- Want to look around first? Tap **Explore without signing in** on the welcome screen.
