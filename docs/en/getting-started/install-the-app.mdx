---

CWA GO runs as an app on iPhone and Android, and in any web browser. Your account is the same everywhere.

## On your phone

1. Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
2. Search for **Cycling Without Age** and install it.
3. Open the app and [sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in).
4. Allow notifications when your phone asks.

## In the browser

Open [cwa.codingforchange.com](https://cwa.codingforchange.com) and sign in. This works on a phone, tablet or computer.

<Note>
Push notifications only work in the app. In the browser, news reaches you by email instead.
</Note>

## Good to know

- Passkeys work in the app too. Add one under [Your account](/en/getting-started/your-account) and skip the code next time.
- Blocked notifications by mistake? Allow them for Cycling Without Age in your phone's settings.
