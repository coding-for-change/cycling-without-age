When someone stops volunteering, you remove them from your chapter. Their account stays, only their access to your chapter goes.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-remove-member.mp4" />

## Steps

1. Open **Members** and click the person's name under **Everyone in the chapter**.
2. Under **Roles**, click **Remove from chapter**.
3. Click **Remove from chapter** again to confirm.

You see "Tobias Brandl has left the chapter." and the person is gone from the list.

## What happens to their rides

- Rides they already rode stay on the record.
- On upcoming rides they show as **No longer a pilot**. Those rides count as short of a pilot again, so you get the [unstaffed warning](/en/chapter-admins/staffing) if nobody steps in.
- They leave your chapter's ride chats.

<Note>
You can't remove yourself, and a chapter can't lose its last admin. Make someone else chapter admin first.
</Note>

## Good to know

- Only want to take away admin rights? Use **Take admin rights** instead. See [Pilots and invitations](/en/chapter-admins/pilots-and-invites).
- Deleting a whole account is for superadmins only. See [Delete a chapter, country or person](/en/organisation/deleting).
