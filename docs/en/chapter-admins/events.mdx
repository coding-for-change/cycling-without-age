An event ride is an outing for a group, often with several trishaws. You can let the chapter's riders book a seat themselves.

## Plan the event

1. Open **Rides**, click **Schedule a ride** and pick **Event ride**.
2. Type the **Event title** and a **Description**: what's planned, where to meet, what to bring.
3. Set the date, start and end in the time row.
4. Under **Where it starts**, pick the place.
5. Click **Add trishaws** and tick every trishaw you need.
6. Set **Pilots needed** with + and −.
7. Enter the **Capacity**: how many riders can come.
8. Press ⌘↵ to schedule the ride.

On the event's page, add up to 6 **Photos**. Riders see them when they book.

## Let riders book

1. On the event's page, find **Riders can book** on the right.
2. Switch on **Show to the chapter's riders**.

You can also switch on **Riders can book** in the drawer before you schedule. The chapter's riders now see the event on their start screen and book seats until it's full. See [Book a seat at an event](/en/riders/book-an-event).

## Good to know

- You can still book riders yourself with **Add a rider** or **Book a rider**, take them off, and drag them into a different order.
- The event's chat holds the pilots and the chapter's admins. Riders' accounts are not in it, because families booking on their own don't know each other.
- Only event rides have photos and can be shown to riders.
