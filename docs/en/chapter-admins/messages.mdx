**Messages** in the sidebar holds your chats: one-to-one, groups you start, and a chat for every ride.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-chat-group.mp4" />

## Start a group chat

1. Open **Messages** and click **New**.
2. Switch to **Group**.
3. Type a **Group name**, for example "Saturday crew".
4. Under **Add people**, type a name and pick the person. Repeat for everyone.
5. Click **Create group**.
6. Type in **Write a message** and press Enter, or click the send button.

## Write to one person

1. Click **New** and stay on **Person**.
2. Type their name, email or phone number and pick them.
3. Click **Start chat**.

You can write to anyone who shares a chapter with you.

## Ride chats

Every ride gets its own chat once the first pilot is on. It holds the pilots, the riders' accounts and the chapter's admins, and follows the ride's crew by itself. On an event, it holds only the pilots and the admins.

<Note>
A group needs you and at least two other people, and holds up to 1,000. Above 100 people, only you can write. Everyone else reads along.
</Note>

## Good to know

- Shift+Enter starts a new line. Enter sends.
- **Unreads** shows only chats with new messages. **Search names and groups** finds a chat.
- A message can be up to 4,000 characters.
