Every trishaw lives at a storage location. Pilots see the location's address, entrance and access code when they fetch and return the trishaw.

## Add a storage location

1. Open **Locations** in the sidebar and click **New location**.
2. Type a **Name**, for example "Garage behind the care home".
3. In **Address**, type the street and pick the result. Drag the pin to fine-tune.
4. Fill in **How to find the entrance**. Add a **Photo of the entrance** if you can.
5. Enter the **Access code** of the door or key safe.
6. Add **Access notes** and **When bringing it back**, for example "Plug in the charger and close the gate."
7. Click **Add location**.

The location's page opens. Change any field there later with its pencil.

<Note>
Only admins and pilots see the access code. Riders never do.
</Note>

## Add a trishaw

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-new-trishaw.mp4" />

1. Open **Trishaws** and click **New trishaw**.
2. Give it a **Name** the pilots will recognise.
3. Enter the **Frame number** stamped on the frame.
4. Under **Model**, search and pick the model.
5. Under **Location**, pick where it is kept.
6. Add a **Photo** and a **Description**, for example its colour or a rain cover.
7. Click **Add trishaw**.

<Warning>
Check the frame number before you save. It can't be changed later.
</Warning>

## Status and week plan

A trishaw's **Status** is **Ready**, **In the workshop** or **Retired**. Change it under **About** on the trishaw's page. Only **Ready** trishaws can be booked.

The **Week plan** tab shows every trishaw's rides, week by week. Use it to see which trishaw is free.

## Good to know

- No fitting model? Models are shared by chapter, country or everywhere. See [Share trishaw models](/en/organisation/models).
- Sharing a depot with other chapters? Under **Locations**, click **Join a pool**, enter the **Pool code** from your country admin and click **Send request**. See [Run a trishaw pool](/en/organisation/pools).
- Your chapter's default location can't be removed. Another location can be archived once its trishaws have moved.
