You plan every ride on the **Rides** calendar. Pick the ride type, the time, a trishaw, the riders and the pilots.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-schedule-ride.mp4" />

## Steps

1. Open **Rides** and click **Schedule a ride**.
2. Pick the ride type at the top: **Event ride**, **Pleasure ride** or **Functional ride**.
3. Click the time row. Pick the date, then type the start time and press Enter. The end follows.
4. Under **Where it starts**, pick the care home or type an address.
5. Click **Add trishaws** and pick one.
6. Click **Add passengers** and tick the riders.
7. Click **Add pilots** and tick a pilot, or leave it open for pilots to [sign up themselves](/en/chapter-admins/staffing).
8. Something the pilots should know, like the entrance? Click **Note for pilots** and write it.
9. Click **Schedule ride**, or press ⌘↵.

The ride's page opens and the ride is on the calendar.

## The three ride types

- **Pleasure ride**: a ride for the joy of it, back where it began. 1 pilot, 1 trishaw, up to 2 riders.
- **Functional ride**: from A to B, for example to the doctor. Pick **Where it goes**, up to 2 riders. Switch on **Round trip** and set the **Time at the destination**: the way back becomes its own ride.
- **Event ride**: an outing for a group, often with several trishaws. It has a title and its own capacity. See [Run an event](/en/chapter-admins/events).

<Note>
Only trishaws that are free for the whole ride can be picked. Trishaws from a pool carry a **Pool** badge.
</Note>

## Plan on the calendar

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-calendar-drag.mp4" />

- Drag down over an empty spot to mark a time. The **New ride** drawer opens with that date and time filled in. A single click works too.
- Drag a ride to another time or another day of the week. You see **Ride moved** with **Undo**.
- Drag the bottom edge of a ride to change when it ends.
- Switch between **Calendar** and **List** at the top. The list shows the rides day by day.

## Good to know

- Moving the way there of a round trip moves the way back along.
- A ride lasts at least 15 minutes and at most 12 hours.
- Changing a ride later works on its page. See [Change or cancel a ride](/en/chapter-admins/change-or-cancel).
