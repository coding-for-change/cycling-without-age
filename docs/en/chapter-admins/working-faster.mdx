Most admin work needs only a few keys. Here is how to find anything, edit in place and undo a slip.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-command-bar.mp4" />

## Find anything with ⌘K

1. Press **⌘K** on a Mac, or **Ctrl+K** on Windows. The bottom of the sidebar reminds you: **⌘ K for anything**.
2. Type what you're looking for, for example "trishaw".
3. Pick an entry with the mouse, or with ↑, ↓ and Enter.

The list has **Create** (for example **Schedule a ride**, **Add a trishaw**, **Join a trishaw pool**), **Go to** for every page, **Perspective** and **Account**. Everyday words work too: "bike", "damage", "garage" or "access code" find the right page. Esc or ⌘K again closes it.

**⌘B** (Ctrl+B) shows or hides the sidebar.

## Edit in place

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-inline-edit-undo.mp4" />

On a detail page, such as a ride, a trishaw or a location, you change a field right where it is.

1. Click the field or its pencil.
2. Type the change.
3. Press Enter, or click somewhere else. In a longer text box, press **⌘↵** (Ctrl+Enter).

It saves straight away. The header shows **Saving**, then **Saved**. A toast says **Saved.** with an **Undo** button. Click **Undo** and you see **Put back.** Press Esc while typing to drop the edit.

## Drawers

New things open in a drawer on the right, on a phone from the bottom.

- **⌘↵** (Ctrl+Enter) sends the form, for example **Schedule ride ⌘↵**.
- Each drawer has its own link, such as `/admin/rides?new=1`. Bookmark it, or send it to a co-admin.
- Esc or closing the drawer throws away what you typed. Nothing is kept as a draft.

<Warning>
Deleting something for good asks you to type a word, for example `DELETE-17-10-2026` for a ride or `DELETE-FOHN` for the trishaw Föhn. Upper or lower case doesn't matter. The red box above it lists what goes with it.
</Warning>

## Good to know

- Sections on the right of a detail page fold away with their chevron.
- The History on a detail page records every change, including an undo.
