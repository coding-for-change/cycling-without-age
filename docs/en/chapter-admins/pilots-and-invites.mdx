New pilots ask to join your chapter, or you invite them yourself. Both happen under **Members**.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-approve-pilot.mp4" />

## Approve or turn down a pilot

1. Open **Members** in the sidebar. Applications wait under **Waiting for approval**.
2. In the person's row, click **Approve** or **Turn down**.
3. Add a note if you like, for example a training date or a phone number.
4. Click **Approve** (or **Turn down**) at the bottom of the drawer.

The person gets an email either way. An approved pilot gets the three steps to their first ride and shows up under **Everyone in the chapter**. Someone turned down can ask another chapter.

## Invite someone

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-invite-member.mp4" />

1. On **Members**, click **Invite someone**.
2. Fill in **Their name** and **Email address**.
3. Under **Role**, tick **Pilot**, **Chapter admin**, or both.
4. Click **Send the invitation**.

They get an email with a link to join and appear in the list straight away.

<Note>
**Invite someone** only shows when one chapter is picked in the sidebar. See [Switch between chapters](/en/organisation/scopes).
</Note>

## Make someone an admin, or take it back

1. On **Members**, click the person's name.
2. Under **Roles**, click **Make chapter admin**, or **Take admin rights**.
3. Confirm.

A chapter admin can approve pilots, book rides and see everyone's details. Someone whose admin rights you take stays in the chapter.

## Good to know

- You can't change your own role. Ask another admin.
- A chapter can't lose its last admin. Add another one first.
- Does the person already have a CWA GO account? The invitation adds the roles to it.
- The note on an approval can be up to 500 characters.
