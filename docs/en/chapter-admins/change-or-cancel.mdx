Plans change. You edit a ride on its page, cancel it when it can't go ahead, and delete a cancelled ride if you need the slot.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-cancel-ride.mp4" />

## Change a ride

Click the ride on the calendar. On its page, click any field and change it: date, start, duration, place, trishaws, riders, pilots or the **Note for pilots**. Each change saves at once, with **Undo** in the toast. See [Work faster in the admin](/en/chapter-admins/working-faster).

## Cancel a ride

1. Open the ride and click **Cancel ride** at the right end of the header.
2. Under **Why is it off?**, pick a reason: **Weather**, **Rider cancelled**, **Care home or facility**, **No pilot available**, **Trishaw problem**, **No riders** or **Something else**.
3. Add a **Note**, for example "Thunderstorms forecast".
4. On a round trip, tick **Also cancel the way back** if that is off too.
5. Click **Cancel ride**.

The ride stays on the calendar, struck through. Its trishaws are free for other rides again. The reasons show up under **Cancellations** in [Reports](/en/chapter-admins/reports).

<Note>
Everyone on the ride is told: riders, pilots and the other admins. A cancellation always goes out by email too.
</Note>

## Delete a cancelled ride

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-delete-ride.mp4" />

1. Open the cancelled ride and click **Delete ride** in the header.
2. Read the red box. It lists what goes with the ride, for example "1 rider booking".
3. Type the word shown, for example `DELETE-17-10-2026`.
4. Click **Delete ride**.

The ride is gone from the calendar and from every report. Riders, pilots and trishaws stay.

<Warning>
A deleted ride can't be brought back. On a round trip, cancel the other leg first. Deleting is blocked while it still goes ahead.
</Warning>

## Good to know

- Only a cancelled ride can be deleted.
- A cancelled ride can't be changed any more. Only its **History** still takes comments.
