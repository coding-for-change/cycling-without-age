Damage reports come from pilots after a ride, or from you. A grounded trishaw stays off the road until an admin clears the damage.

## Report damage

1. Open **Trishaws** and click the trishaw.
2. In the **Damage** box, click **Report damage**.
3. Under **What is wrong?**, describe the problem. Add a **Photo** if it helps.
4. If riding it would be unsafe, switch on **Ground this trishaw**.
5. Click **Send report**.

A grounded trishaw gets a red **Grounded** badge and its status switches to **In the workshop**. It can't be booked. Without grounding, the report is for information only: the trishaw stays bookable and the list shows **Damage reported**.

Pilots report damage in the app when they [finish a ride](/en/pilots/finish-your-ride).

<Note>
Grounding damage always alerts every admin of the chapter. Minor damage only reaches you while **Minor damage reaches you too** is on in **Settings**.
</Note>

## Clear damage

1. On the trishaw's page, find the report in **History** and click **Clear**.
2. Under **What happened?**, write what was done, for example "New brake cable fitted".
3. Click **Clear damage**.
4. Check **Status** under **About**. It should read **Ready** again. If not, pick **Ready**.

## Good to know

- A trishaw with open grounding damage can't be set to **Ready**. Clear the damage first.
- A trishaw you set to **Retired** stays retired when you clear its damage.
- Cleared reports stay in the History, with your note.
