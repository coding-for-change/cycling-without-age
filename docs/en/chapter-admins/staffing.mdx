A ride needs its pilots. You put them on yourself, or let pilots take open rides on their own.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-staff-ride.mp4" />

## Put a pilot on a ride

1. On the **Rides** calendar, click the ride.
2. In the **Pilots** section on the right, click **Put a pilot on** (the + next to the count).
3. Search by name and click the pilot.

You see "Piet Pilot is on the ride." The **Unstaffed** badge goes once the ride has all the pilots it needs. The pilot is told. To take them off again, click the × next to their name.

## Let pilots sign up themselves

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-settings-staffing.mp4" />

1. Open **Settings** and scroll to **Staffing**.
2. Switch on **Pilots sign up themselves**.
3. Under **Warn about unstaffed rides**, pick how early you want to hear about a ride still short of pilots: **12 hours before**, **1 day before**, **2 days before**, **3 days before** or **7 days before**. The default is 2 days.

With sign-up on, your pilots are told about new rides that still need a pilot. They take a seat without asking. See [Sign up for a ride](/en/pilots/sign-up-for-a-ride).

<Warning>
The warning time is also the staffing window. From then on, pilots can't step back from the ride in the app. They have to call you. See [Step back from a ride](/en/pilots/stepping-back).
</Warning>

## Good to know

- You get one alert per ride that is still short of pilots when the staffing window opens.
- **Pilots per ride** sets how many pilots a new ride asks for. Change it on a ride under **Pilots needed**.
- The ride's chat opens once the first pilot is on. See [Chat and messages](/en/chapter-admins/messages).
