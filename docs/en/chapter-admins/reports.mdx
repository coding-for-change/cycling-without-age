Reports show what your chapter rode, and how that compares with the period before. Handy when someone asks what your chapter does.

## Read the report

1. Open **Reports** in the sidebar. It opens on the last 30 days.
2. Pick a timeframe: **7D**, **30D**, **90D**, **YTD**, **12M**, **All**, or **Custom** for your own dates.
3. Read the key figures: **Rides**, **Trips**, **Riders**, **Hours** and **Cancellations**. Each compares with the period before.
4. Click a figure to show it in the chart.
5. Click a bar in the chart to zoom in on that day, week or month. The whole page follows.

A trip is one outing. **Rides** counts one ride per rider on a trip.

## Further down

- **Leaderboard**: your top **Pilots** and **Riders** by **Rides** or **Hours**. Click **See all** for the full table, sortable by any column.
- **Where the rides happened**: a map with each chapter as a bubble, sized by its rides.
- **Cancellations**: how many trips were cancelled, and why.
- **Ride models**: how rides split across event, pleasure and functional rides.
- **People**: active and inactive pilots and riders.

## Good to know

- The tiles on **Overview** show the last 30 days against the 30 before. Click one to open it here.
- Active in **People** means at least one ride in the last 12 months, whatever timeframe you picked.
- There is no export. Share the page link instead: it keeps the timeframe and the zoom.
- Looking after several chapters? See [Compare chapters in reports](/en/organisation/reports).
