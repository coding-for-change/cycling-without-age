Work through this list once and your chapter is ready to ride. Each step links to its own guide.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-settings-staffing.mp4" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in Settings">
    Open **Settings** in the sidebar. Every change saves on its own.

    - **A word of welcome**: a few lines that every new pilot and passenger gets in their welcome mail.
    - **Reply-to address**: answers to your chapter's mail land here. Riders also see a **Write an email** card under **My rides** only when this is set. See [Ask for a ride](/en/riders/ask-for-a-ride).
    - **Notifications**: decide who hears about new members, pilot applications and minor damage.
    - **Post-ride instructions** under **After the ride**: what pilots see when they finish a ride.
    - **Staffing**: how pilots find rides and when you hear about a ride still short of pilots. See [Staff a ride](/en/chapter-admins/staffing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your storage location">
    Open **Locations** and click your chapter's location. Check the **Access code** and **When bringing it back**, so pilots can get in and park the trishaw properly. See [Locations and trishaws](/en/chapter-admins/locations-and-trishaws).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your trishaws">
    Open **Trishaws** and click **New trishaw** for each one. See [Locations and trishaws](/en/chapter-admins/locations-and-trishaws).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the join link">
    The **Join link** card in **Settings** has **Copy link**, **Print the poster** and **Save QR code as PNG**. Hang the poster in the care home and send the link to volunteers. Riders join as in [Join as a rider](/en/riders/join), pilots as in [Join a chapter as a pilot](/en/pilots/join-a-chapter).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or invite pilots">
    Open **Members**. Approve the people under **Waiting for approval**, or click **Invite someone**. See [Pilots and invitations](/en/chapter-admins/pilots-and-invites).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add passengers">
    Open **Passengers** and click **Add a passenger** for residents who don't use a phone. See [Add passengers](/en/chapter-admins/passengers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the first ride">
    Open **Rides** and click **Schedule a ride**. See [Schedule a ride](/en/chapter-admins/schedule-a-ride).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Do you look after several chapters? Pick one chapter in the sidebar first. Settings, **Invite someone** and **Add a passenger** only show for a single chapter. See [Switch between chapters](/en/organisation/scopes).
</Tip>

## Good to know

- There is no Save button in **Settings**. You see **Saved** and can take a change back with **Undo** in the toast.
- **Pilots per ride** under **Staffing** is the default for new rides. You can change it on each ride.
- Stuck? **Report a problem** at the bottom of **Settings** reaches the people who look after CWA GO.
