Many riders sign up at the door, not on a phone. You add them to your chapter so you can book them on rides.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/admin-add-passenger.mp4" />

## Steps

1. Open **Passengers** in the sidebar and click **Add a passenger**.
2. Fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Birthday** and **Gender**.
3. Enter their **Email or phone number**.
4. Does a relative or carer help them? Tick **Someone helps this person** and fill in **Their name**, **Relationship** and **Their email or phone number**.
5. Click **Add passenger**, or press ⌘↵.

You see "Erna Huber is on the list." and the person appears in the table.

Adding a whole care home? Switch on **Add another right after**. The form stays open and clears for the next resident.

## When the helper holds the account

Some riders have no email or phone of their own. Enter the helper's email or phone number in both places. The drawer then says the account will belong to the helper, who manages the rider. The helper signs in with it and can book event seats for the rider. See [Set up an account for someone else](/en/riders/booking-for-someone-else).

<Note>
Each email or phone number can hold one account. If the passenger's own email or number is already taken, you see "That email or number already has an account." If the helper already has an account, they get a request instead and the passenger is added once they say yes.
</Note>

## Link a carer to a rider who has their own account

A rider who signed up on their own can still get a carer who books for them.

1. Open the rider from **Passengers**.
2. Under **Carer**, click **Link a carer…**.
3. Pick the carer. They need to be a member of the rider's chapter.
4. Click **Send request**, or press ⌘↵.

The carer gets an email and a notification and answers it in the app. Until then the rider's page shows "Waiting for … to answer", and nothing changes. The rider keeps their own account and still sees their rides.

A rider without an account of their own already has someone who books for them. To move them to someone else, use **Hand over…** on their page.


## Good to know

- The person can take the account over later. They sign in with the same email or phone number. See [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in).
- **Add a passenger** only shows when one chapter is picked in the sidebar.
- Riders who join on their own phone show up in this list by themselves.
