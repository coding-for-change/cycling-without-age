Many riders don't use a phone. A family member or carer can hold the account for them and book their seats.

## The easy way: let your chapter set it up

Ask your chapter to add the person you care for, with **your** email or phone number as the helper's contact. The account is then created for you, already linked to them. [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in) with that email or number and you're done.

## On your own

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="mx-auto w-full max-w-xs rounded-3xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/rider-caretaker.mp4" />

1. [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in) with your own email address or phone number.
2. Tap **I want to be a Passenger**.
3. Under **Where should we pick you up?**, choose **Care home** or **My own address**, pick the chapter and tap **Next**.
4. Tick the safety agreement and tap **Continue**.
5. On **A little about you**, tap **I'm creating this account for someone else to ride**.
6. Under **Your relationship to them**, pick how you know them, for example **Son or daughter**. Tap **Continue**.
7. Tap **Create passkey**, or **Not now**.

<Warning>
The app can't add the person you care for to an account you made yourself yet. Your chapter can't link them afterwards either. If you can, use the easy way above.
</Warning>

## Good to know

- Once the person you care for is on your account, you can [book event seats](/en/riders/book-an-event) for them and see their [rides](/en/riders/your-rides).
- Pilots call you for these riders. Their ride page shows your name next to the rider's.
