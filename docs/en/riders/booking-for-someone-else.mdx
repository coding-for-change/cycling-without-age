Many riders don't use a phone. A family member or carer can hold the account for them and book their seats.

## Sign up for them

1. [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in) with your own email address or phone number.
2. Tap **I'm booking for someone else**.
3. Under **Which chapter should they ride with?**, pick the chapter and tap **Next**. Came from your chapter's poster or join link? Then the chapter is already picked and this step is skipped.
4. Tick the agreements and tap **Continue**.
5. On **Who will ride?**, check your name under **About you**. Pick **Your relationship to them** if you like, for example **Son or daughter**.
6. Tap **Add a person who will ride**. Fill in their name, birthday and gender.
7. Under **Where should we pick them up?**, choose **Care home**, or **Their own address** and pick the address from the list.
8. Under **Riding needs**, switch on **Needs a wheelchair bike** if they need one, and leave a **Note for pilots** if it helps. Both are optional.
9. Tap **Done**. Add anyone else with **Add another person**, then tap **Continue with 1 person**.
10. Tap **Create passkey**, or **Not now**.

## Add someone later

1. Open your profile and go to **People you look after**.
2. Tap **Add another person** and fill in the same details as above.
3. Tap **Add person**.

Everyone you look after rides with the same chapter, and you can look after up to 10 people.

## When your chapter asks you

Your chapter can also ask you to look after someone, for example a resident who already rides with them. You get an email and a notification. Open the request and tap **Yes, I'll look after** them, or **No, thanks**. Nothing changes until you answer.

If you don't have an account yet, ask your chapter to add the person you care for with **your** email or phone number as the helper's contact. The account is then created for you, already linked to them. [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in) with that email or number and you're done.

## Good to know

- Once the person you care for is on your account, you can [book event seats](/en/riders/book-an-event) for them and see their [rides](/en/riders/your-rides).
- Pilots call you for these riders. Their ride page shows your name next to the rider's.
- Someone who has their own account keeps it when you start looking after them, and still sees their rides.
