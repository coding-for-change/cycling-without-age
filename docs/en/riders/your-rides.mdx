## Steps

1. Tap **My rides** in the bottom bar.
2. Under **Coming up**, tap your ride.
3. Tap **Open in maps** to see where the ride starts.
4. Under **Your pilot**, tap the call button to phone your pilot.
5. Tap **Message everyone on this ride** to write to the pilot and your chapter.

## Good to know

- Until a pilot is on the ride, **Your pilot** reads **Not assigned yet**. Your chapter is finding one.
- On a round trip, the page shows when you come back. The way back has its own ride page.
- Questions about a ride? **Your chapter** at the bottom of the page shows who to ask.
