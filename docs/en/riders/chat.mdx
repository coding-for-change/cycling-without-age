Every ride has a chat with your pilot and your chapter. You find all your chats under **Chat**.

## Write on a ride

1. Open the ride under **My rides**.
2. Tap **Message everyone on this ride**.
3. Tap **Write a message**, type, and tap **Send**.

## Start a new chat

1. Tap **Chat** in the bottom bar, then **New**.
2. Search by name, email or phone number and tap **Start chat**.

<Note>
You can only start a chat with people who share a chapter with you.
</Note>

## Good to know

- The ride chat opens once a pilot is on the ride.
- Event chats are for the pilots and admins only.
- A message can be up to 4000 characters.
