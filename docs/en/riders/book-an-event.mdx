Some events are open to riders. You book seats yourself until they're full.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="mx-auto w-full max-w-xs rounded-3xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/rider-book-event.mp4" />

## Steps

1. On Home, under **Upcoming events**, tap the event.
2. Under **Book a seat**, tick who's coming.
3. Tap **Book 2 seats** (the number follows how many you ticked).

The page now shows **Who's riding** and **Your pilot**.

<Note>
Each event shows how many seats are left. You can't pick more riders than that.
</Note>

## Good to know

- Only events your chapter opened to riders appear here.
- If you only book for yourself, you see your own name and nobody else.
- Seats left after booking? Use **Bring someone else along** on the event page.
