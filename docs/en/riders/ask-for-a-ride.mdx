You can't book a regular ride in the app. Your chapter plans it with you and puts it in your rides.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="mx-auto w-full max-w-xs rounded-3xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/rider-ask-ride.mp4" />

## Steps

1. Tap **My rides** in the bottom bar.
2. Under **No rides planned right now**, find the card that asks your chapter for a ride. Tap **Write an email**.
3. Write when you'd like to go out, and where to. Send the email.

<Note>
The card only shows when your chapter has set up an email address for riders, and while you have no rides coming up.
</Note>

Your chapter finds a day, a time and a pilot. The ride then shows under [Your rides](/en/riders/your-rides).

## Good to know

- No card? Contact your chapter directly. **Your chapter** on Home shows its address.
- [Events](/en/riders/book-an-event) are the exception: those you can book yourself.
