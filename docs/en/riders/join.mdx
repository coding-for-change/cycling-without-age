Your chapter's poster has a QR code. It takes you straight to your chapter, so joining takes a few minutes.

<video autoPlay muted loop playsInline className="mx-auto w-full max-w-xs rounded-3xl border border-gray-200 dark:border-gray-800" src="/videos/rider-join-qr.mp4" />

## Steps

1. Scan the QR code on the poster, or open the join link your chapter sent you.
2. Under **Take part with this chapter**, tap **Get started**.
3. Enter your email address or phone number and tap **Continue**.
4. Type the 6-digit code you receive.
5. Tap **I want to be a Passenger**. The chapter from the poster is already picked.
6. Tick the safety agreement and tap **Continue**.
7. Fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Birthday** and **Gender**, then tap **Continue**.
8. Tap **Create passkey**, or **Not now** to do it later.

Your Home now shows your next ride, events you can book and **Your chapter**.

<Note>
The agreement is about safety: rides are given by trained volunteers, and you agree to your chapter's safety guidelines.
</Note>

## Good to know

- No poster at hand? [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in), tap **I want to be a Passenger**, and pick your chapter under **Where should we pick you up?**
- Setting this up for a parent or someone you care for? Tap **I'm booking for someone else** in step 5 instead. See [Booking for someone else](/en/riders/booking-for-someone-else).
